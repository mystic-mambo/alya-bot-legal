Alya The Security Bot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Store

Alya The Security Bot may store information necessary to provide moderation and server-management features.

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord server IDs
  • Moderation warnings
  • Moderation strikes
  • Moderation reasons
  • Moderator IDs
  • Moderation timestamps
  • Moderation history and statistics

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide moderation functionality, maintain moderation history, prevent abuse, and operate the bot.

3. Information Sharing

Alya does not intentionally sell personal information. Information stored by the bot is not intentionally shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the service or where required by law.

4. Data Retention

Moderation-related information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the bot's moderation features.

5. Data Deletion

Users or server administrators may request deletion of information associated with Alya's stored moderation data by contacting the bot owner.

6. Discord

Alya operates through Discord. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

7. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Alya's features or data practices change.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the bot owner through the support method provided by the bot owner.
